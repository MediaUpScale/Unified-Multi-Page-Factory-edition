His other fist is clenched behind his back. Freezing rain slashes sideways; he does not stop. The earth is devastated — rusted mega-gears half-buried in mud, broken clockwork towers, scattered cogwheels and industrial wreckage in every direction, hanging cable bundles and cybernetic conduits through industrial haze. Full body, grit, high contrast, no pause in the drill.
